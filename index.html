What is people whacking?甚麼是打小人？

「小人」泛指暗中作對的人或事，例如背後中傷的同事、惹是生非的鄰居，或接連不斷的霉運。由打小人師傅手持舊鞋，拍打寫上名字的紙公仔，口中唸唸有詞，把晦氣趕走。打小人已列入香港非物質文化遺產清單。

Da siu yan (打小人), literally “hitting petty people,” is a Cantonese folk ritual. A “petty person” is anyone or anything working against you: a backstabbing colleague, a gossiping neighbour, a streak of bad luck.

You write the name of the villain on a paper figure, then a practitioner, usually called a villain hitter, beats it with an old shoe while chanting rhymes that send the bad energy packing. It is loud, theatrical, and surprisingly cathartic.

The practice is part of Hong Kong’s intangible cultural heritage inventory.

The essentials · 重點資料

Where · 地點
Traditionally under the Canal Road Flyover at Goose Neck Bridge (鵝頸橋), between Wan Chai and Causeway Bay.
灣仔與銅鑼灣之間的鵝頸橋底。
When · 時間
Year-round, but busiest on Jingzhe (驚蟄), the “awakening of insects” in early March, when villains are said to stir.
全年皆可，驚蟄（約3月5日）前後最旺。
Who · 對象
Anyone. Locals, visitors, and the very curious.
任何人都可以：街坊、遊客，或單純好奇的你。

How a session goes儀式流程

  1. Make offerings上香祭拜

    Incense, candles and paper money are offered to the gods to ask for their help.

  2. Name your villain寫下小人

    Their name, or your trouble, goes on a paper figure. No names? A generic villain works too.

  3. Whack拍打小人

    The practitioner strikes the figure with a shoe, chanting as it goes. You are welcome to join in.

  4. Burn it化寶送走

    The paper villain is burned, and the white tiger is soothed with pork fat so it won’t turn on you.
